## 2. CHECKABLE OBSTRUCTIONS TO SCALING (IN PRIORITY ORDER)

### G1 — Two-episode structure and hard-coded 16-beat policy, not compositional N-form

In kernel.rs:
- longform_domain rejects all totals except 2.0 * EPISODE_BEATS, requires exactly two placements, two theme sites and the precise Establish→Consequent relation.
- Each placement must occupy exactly [0,16) or [16,32); the route requires hard-coded dominant/minor arrivals in bars 3, 4 and 8.
- perform_song derives episodes from total_beats/EPISODE_BEATS AFTER that strict validation; its score length is hardcoded at 2*EPISODE_BEATS.
- Keys reply lives only in a second-bar [episode_start+4, episode_start+8) window, the drummer's keys echo lives at episode_start+10.5, and the final pulse/section fills depend on a modulo-four bar index.
- pad is omitted; current example supplies no bridge/transformative return.

These restrictions are fine for a bounded proof, but merely relaxing the length guard, copy-pasting the two episodes, or stacking WAVs will destroy the meaningful source-specific variation. The next implementation must allow variable section spans, source-driven response windows, and transition duties while retaining one continuous Score and synthesizer state.

### G2 — The source-bound memory is not rich enough to distinguish causal debts

BandMemory currently holds only open_call: bool and prev_answer_events: usize. The latter is assigned but has NO readers (a dead representational promise), and the remembered source is one adjacent previous InteractionMaterial variable (carried_cell). If two different calls are unanswered successively in a future 3+-episode song, the older source will silently be overwritten. An unpaid question must remain bound to a particular source material / action / episode with an explicit settlement, deferral or intentionally-open ending.

Worse, a NOW-ACTIVE two-episode provenance mismatch exists in the mute-first-keys control:
- In episode e=1, if memory.open_call, transform_material correctly transforms the PREVIOUS episode's carried_cell.
- But the Answer action is created with perf.statements[e].call, the CURRENT episode's call ID.
- The notes therefore *musically* quote the original unanswered verse while the official ActionCause::Interaction is credited to the current hook. This is a source/provenance semantic mismatch, not merely a future scalability risk. Fix the action/cue relationship to point to the actual paid originating obligation, with a read-only independent witness.

AND: source_plan initializes InteractionPlan.interactions to an empty Vec. perform_song creates answer Actions and writes keys note provenance.interaction=Some(InteractionId(e)), but never adds corresponding Interaction objects to perf.interactions. The source-level causal ledger is thus not closed under referential identity, even if the Score audio is plausible. Current Score.validate focuses on time/pitch/velocity rather than plan/interaction foreign-key integrity. Give every sound-bearing response an actual matching source/interaction/call relationship or remove the dishonest reference and type an intentional omission.

These facts were confirmed by exact GitHub source inspection; no new Rust test was executed by this researcher. Red-first tests must exercise the existing mute-first-keys scenario and prove that references exist and point to the RIGHT originating source/action before editing the implementation.

### G3 — "One pocket" has been conflated with "the same expression for every musician"

The current pocket_offset(position in bar) is shared by the lead source and bass/drum events; it is a valid common phase reference, but every on-grid instrument offset is largely identical by position. Keys' four-event response uses the exact grid. Better coordination does not require exact micro-offset equality, and earlier CoupledR8 was ear-rejected after forcing too much agreement.

Correct model: one metric coordinate phi(t), with role-specific bounded source-justified relative phase delta_i(t), preserved and auditable across sections. At a transition, the bassist can anticipate a future chord, keys can intentionally lay behind the snare, and the drummer can keep an invariant anchor; their relative phases must not jump/reset without a declared source event. NEVER random-walk timing or resurrect the original 3/2 ratio pocket drift.

### G4 — Macro-dynamics reset mechanically at the seam

kernel.rs::groove_swell(bar, bars_per_episode) = 0.94 + 0.12 * ((bar % bars_per_episode)/bars_per_episode); with 4 bars/episode, the multiplier traces 0.94, 0.97, 1.00, 1.03, then snaps back to 0.94 exactly when the hook starts. Bass and kit both use it alongside shared harmonic-drive gain.

A phase change may legitimately involve a sudden downbeat/pullback, but this policy creates the SAME reset for every section whether the source story demands a drop, a surge, continued propulsion or a silent suspense. Rhythmically seamless does NOT mean globally constant intensity; it means the next section's change was prepared and player-specific. Replace unconditional modulo resetting with source-owned transition intent and role-specific arrival/recovery, while protecting the groove anchors.

### G5 — Harmonic "Home" depends on root alone: future bridge/pivot bug

kernel.rs::harmonic_drive first returns Home if chord.root_pc == tonic_pc, BEFORE inspecting Quality::Dom7/Quality::Dom9. On the larger C137 BRIDGE_PROG, an A7 (tonic-root dominant seventh, acting as V7/iv) is an ACTIVE secondary dominant and should not automatically be classified as tonic rest merely because it shares root A. This error is not exercised by the current eight-bar Am minor verse/hook but will become musically consequential as soon as a real pivoting bridge is added.

Use an actual source-relative harmonic FUNCTION / destination / leading-tone/resolution obligation, not just tonic pitch-class. Distinguish at least a static tonic minor from a tonic-root secondary dominant, and test the different expected bass, drum, harmonic and response behavior. The source already owns contextual_function and destination-aware chord routes; reuse them. Do not impose a new hand-typed seven-chord lookup.

### G6 — The lead is forcibly legato across every episode seam

perform_song constructs an explicit VoiceContinuation from the last verse note to the first hook note at the 16-beat seam, saying they are "one voice" and avoiding unwanted mono release tails. This is legitimate for a deliberately continuous/tied entrance, but the code does so unconditionally. A new section may intentionally require an accentuated new attack, silence, a breath, a pickup or a continuation. Voice identity (same instrument) is NOT sufficient evidence for legato continuity across a THEMATIC boundary.

Make articulation across the transition an explicit decision based on source, performed note endpoints, voice capacity and intended musical action. A blanket same-role link can obscure a phase change or suppress its expressive attack. Retain the original seam as a positive legato control, but add contrasting required-detach and intentional-overlap/refusal tests.

### G7 — Section provenance and observability aren't truthful on the new performance

The generic kernel provenance(perf, at, label) constructs Provenance::new(SectionKind::A) and discards perf and at. The long-form accompaniment and drums therefore claim section A even in the hook, unless later overridden by another path (none found in this kernel's emitters). Score.sections is never assigned by kernel::perform_song, despite Score having the field. Required lead source provenance is handled by source transport and should remain audited separately.

Section metadata alone doesn't make music, but false provenance prevents a rigorous observer from connecting local musician action to the actual form/phase-change cause. Derive section/phrase/role source identity from the final actual song/plan at event onset BEFORE committing events, not in a post hoc stamping pass. Give every action, note and drum fill an auditable section/causal source link.

### G8 — Repetition and true information density remain insufficiently tested

The 32-beat test suite (seeds 2112/7/99, A-Aeolian BLACK_ICE) tests source variation, >=60 lead notes, bass vs home density, snare layback, distinct backbeat velocities and some ordinary comp holes. These prove meaningful necessary properties.

They do NOT prove independent instruments develop differently across forms, bridge obligation/return, phrase-to-phrase memory beyond one bool, no reset of expressive phase at boundaries, non-vacuous source settlement, or preservation of listener-recognizable information as length grows. The source shows that the bass+drum figures currently depend largely on chart/root/quality and absolute bar index. Changing the lead to another genuinely different source with the SAME note onsets/harmony may leave these players mostly/entirely unchanged. The keys use a coarse first-bar rising/closed test plus a last-four-note response. More long-form seeds cannot fix information lost by a coarse decision map.

**Important:** a more varied melody doesn't require random backing. Require differential response ONLY where named source duties or genuine musical knowledge warrants it. A strong steady pocket is a value. Data diversity by hash alone is a bad objective.

## 3. Correct categorical target: an indexed, partial category of musical episodes

### Objects: full boundary contexts, not global loudness

At boundary beat b, use a typed context I_b with components (names illustrative; reuse existing data):
- world-relative pulse clock and metric phase (absolute beat; event/pickup offsets);
- final harmonic region, chord, FUNCTION, expected destination and actual guide-tone responsibilities;
- thematic referents/motif memory and obligations/debts with ACTUAL IDs;
- player-local duty and recent gesture histories, breath/recovery and stage/mono capacity;
- already active/releasing voice tails and authorized transitions (continuation vs detach);
- named written silence and authorized look-ahead/anticipation windows.

### Arrows: musical sections as state-changing processes

An episode is a partial morphism E: I_a -> I_b emitting source-justified, causally committed notes/drums/silences and a final actual context. It may refuse illegal source/harmony/phase/role transitions. Local independence exists inside the shared context, not through a global fader.

The correct *testable* gluing law:

    P(E2 ◦ E1, I0)
      ≃ join_B(P(E1,I0), P(E2,I1)),   where I1 = out(E1) = in(E2).

Here join_B means one global, temporally ordered Score with explicit legal cross-seam continuations and one synthesizer state. The equivalence ≃ is defined on canonical note/drum/source/continuity observations, NOT automatically byte equality of separately mastered PCM. DSP memory, echo/reverb release and compressor state make concatenating two standalone WAVs invalid.

Associativity should hold when well-typed sections are regrouped WITHOUT changing declared musical semantics; "cutting a bar" as an administrative identity must not rewrite a note or re-roll randomness. Musical transitions may intentionally change when the SOURCE section structure changes. Source tags/ref IDs must be conserved or transformed under declared relationship and debt settlement, not magically replaced with "same contour."

### Morphism for an earned "phase change"

A section change should be a source-defined boundary process with a PREPARATION interval before b and a RESPONSE interval after b:
- a motif or rhythmic/harmonic obligation opened/prepared earlier;
- at least one musician anticipates, withholds or alters duty BEFORE the boundary;
- the harmony / mode / thematic register / ensemble orchestration changes at b according to a declared transformation;
- different players respond on their own schedules AFTER b;
- stable pocket anchors, source identities and voice/state continuity survive as required;
- the new section exposes a consequential difference from a boundary where no source transition happened.

An individual instrument need not all swell together. A bassist may lead into the new chord, the kit may hold back a snare, the lead may pause and the keys may arrive late. Common intent does NOT require common amplitude or zero-lag synchronized attacks.

The "phase-change" analogy is phenomenological, not a theorem of physical critical phenomena. Do not add a PDE/turbulence simulator or arbitrary stochastic microtiming.

### Strong tests of this categorical structure

1. **Source identity / foreign-key closure**: every performed answer and claimed InteractionId resolves to a valid actual Interaction, and its ActionCause is the original source call; a quote of earlier verse cannot claim a newer hook initiated it. Test current keys-muted first episode; predict RED under current source.
2. **Persistent multi-debt memory**: two different unanswered calls followed by an answer; no earlier unpaid debt silently overwritten. Correct settlement/refusal/legitimate open ending must be reported by ID. The current bool+carried_cell must be insufficient by construction; treat the 3-episode test as red until generalized.
3. **Repartition invariance**: neutral administrative split of a section into two pieces leaves canonical performed events and player state unchanged, unless the source declares a real musical transition. A source-defined new section can change local policy.
4. **Boundary anticipation causality**: change the destination chord/motif only after the boundary and allow the explicitly licensed PREPARATION window to change while events outside its causal backward horizon remain byte-identical. Remove prep/bridge, preserve tempo and note counts where possible; the transition witness refuses to call the finale equally earned.
5. **Transition articulation**: legato seam, detached seam, true silence seam, pickup seam and voice-capacity conflict have independent positive/negative controls, no unconditional cross-referent choke link.
6. **Functional harmony**: A-minor tonic vs A7 pivot share ROOT but differ in musical function/drive and expected approach; test exact BarChord/HarmonicContext effects and ensure real Score guide tones, not name labels alone.
7. **Pocket across three sections**: preserve absolute beat/phase under administrative rebracketing, permit coherent role-specific lag and recovery; no accumulated drift, off-grid jitter or reset due to episode index.
8. **Independent musician dialogue**: source-altered lead call with same onsets/count + same chart yields an authorized keys/drum response change, or documented justified silence; unrelated bass pulse remains stable unless it actually depends on the cue. Compare role-only and mix PCM.
9. **Transition provenance**: keys response action, interaction, source material, section, phrase, note, velocity and later drum origin all agree and resolve to existing committed IR objects.
10. **Musical information density**: represent *source-backed thematic/harmonic/cue moves per phrase* separately from note onsets. Compare against same-note-count wrong-source, repeated-loop and arbitrary fill mutants. Preserve listener memory and intentional low-density windows. No fabricated semantic-bits score.

## 5. The artistic target in one sentence

The seam is NOT where one locally valid song fragment ends and another begins. The seam is where the band's shared musical information CONTINUES THROUGH A CHANGE OF REGIME. Individual musicians hear the same song from different places, prepare and remember different consequences, and choose what to play—or not play—so the new section makes sense as a consequence of the old one.

A dense C137 performance remains intelligible when the arrangement changes, and grows MORE intelligible because it changed. The final hook belongs to the bridge it just survived, not just the generator's home chord label.
